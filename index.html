A simple path from first message to finished work.

  1. Tell me what you need

    Message me on WhatsApp or email with your goal, deadline and budget.

  2. Agree the plan

    You get a clear scope, timeline and price before work starts.

  3. Build and review

    Work is shared in stages, so you can comment early.

  4. Deliver and hand over

    You receive the finished work with its files and notes.
