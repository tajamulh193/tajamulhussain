FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does setup take?

Paste one script tag, or install the package for your framework. Most teams see their first dashboard in under ten minutes.

Do I need a cookie banner?

Tidewell does not set cookies or store personal data by default, so in many cases no banner is needed. Check with your own legal adviser.

What happens if I go over my event limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep collecting and let you know, and you can move up a tier or pay a small overage.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Every plan includes CSV export, and Scale adds a daily sync to your own warehouse.
