    One oven, one rule

    Everything that leaves the kitchen has touched the fire. Bread is proved overnight and baked at the start of service. Vegetables come from two farms within an hour's drive, and the menu is written the morning we taste them.

    We seat thirty-two people, so a table is a small event. Walk-ins are welcome at the counter.
