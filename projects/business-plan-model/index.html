Executive summary

Households in the twin cities spend an estimated PKR 290 billion a year on groceries, yet fewer than 3% of orders are placed online. Existing apps compete on discounts and deliver in 2 to 4 hours. Doorstep Pantry will run three small dark stores carrying 2,500 everyday items and deliver in 30 minutes, priced in line with neighbourhood stores.

We are raising to open the first three stores, reach operating break-even and build a base of repeat customers. The model below shows the path, and the sensitivity of the result to the four assumptions that matter most.

The plan in six lines

  • Problem: unreliable delivery windows and stock-outs on existing apps.
  • Solution: curated range, own riders, 30-minute promise, live stock.
  • Customer: dual-income households in sectors F, G, E and DHA.
  • Revenue: product margin plus a small delivery fee under PKR 2,000 basket.
  • Moat: order-density per store and private-label staples from Month 12.
  • Team: operations lead, category buyer, product engineer, finance (PhD).

Go-to-market and operations

Launch one store at a time and only open the next when the previous one passes 450 orders a day.

Months 1–6: Prove one store

F-7 store. Referral codes through school and office WhatsApp groups, first-order credit, 30-minute promise or delivery free. Target 60% month-2 retention.

Months 7–18: Three stores

Open DHA and G-11 stores. Shared buying cuts cost of goods by about 2 points. Launch subscriptions for milk, bread and eggs.

Months 19–36: Margin

Private-label staples, supplier-funded promotions, and route batching at peak times to cut delivery cost per order by a fifth.

Key risks and mitigations

Discount war

Larger apps subsidise orders. We compete on reliability, and the model sets aside no discount budget beyond the first order.

Rider costs rise

Fuel is about 40% of delivery cost. A 20% rise lowers NPV by the amount shown in the sensitivity chart; e-bikes in Year 2 are the hedge.

Slow repeat rate

Orders per customer is the biggest driver after basket size. Monthly cohort tracking gives an early warning by Month 3.
