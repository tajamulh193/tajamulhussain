Design decisions

Today is the home screen

People open a habit app to check in, so the list is first and the ring answers "how am I doing" at a glance.

Colour carries meaning

Green means done, amber means a streak, violet means "tap here". Nothing is coloured only for decoration.

Easy to tap, easy to read

Rows are tall enough to tap comfortably, and every state has a label as well as a colour.

Short forms

Adding a habit takes four fields with sensible defaults, so it never feels like setup work.
